runway mode=land || || On land, a fighter jet often uses well over a kilometre of runway.
runway mode=compare || || On a Nimitz carrier, the catapult gives it about ninety metres.
cat mode=slot || THE CATAPULT || So the carrier throws it into the air.
cat mode=track || || Under the deck runs a long track.
cat mode=hook || || The jet's nose wheel is attached to a shuttle that rides in that track.
cat mode=hold || FULL POWER… || The pilot pushes the engines to full power,
cat mode=hold tag=1 zoom=1 || || but the jet doesn't move, because a holdback is holding it in place.
cat mode=jbd || || Behind it, a steel wall rises out of the deck.
cat mode=jbd tag=1 || JET BLAST DEFLECTOR || It's a jet blast deflector,
cat mode=jbd zoom=1 || || and it stops the scorching exhaust from hitting the people and planes behind.

cat mode=shooter || "THE SHOOTER" || Then the catapult officer, nicknamed the shooter, gives the signal.
cat mode=launch || || The holdback lets go, and the catapult fires.
