cat mode=hold || FULL POWER… || The pilot pushes the engines to full power,

# --- 3. Catching a jet with a wire
cockpit view=close || BACK TO THE LANDING || Let's go back to our night landing.
meatball step=1 || THE "MEATBALL" || To stay on the right path down, the pilot watches a light on the side of the deck called the meatball.
meatball step=2 || ON THE GLIDE PATH || If the ball of light sits level with a row of green lights, you're on the glide path.
meatball step=high || TOO HIGH || Too high, and it rises.
meatball step=low || TOO LOW || Too low, and it sinks.
lso || LANDING SIGNAL OFFICER || Beside the landing area stands a landing signal officer,
lso talk=1 || || an experienced pilot who watches every approach and talks the pilots in by radio.
wires mode=top n=4 || ARRESTING WIRES || Across the landing area are thick steel cables, raised a little above the deck.
wires mode=count n=4 || NIMITZ: [4] || Most Nimitz-class carriers have four.
wires mode=count n=3 || FORD: [3] || The Ford, and the two newest Nimitz ships, have three.
wires mode=hook || THE TAILHOOK || Under the tail of the jet hangs a hook.
wires mode=catch || || The pilot flies the plane firmly onto the deck so the hook can catch one of those wires.
wires mode=aim || AIM FOR WIRE [3] || On a four-wire deck, pilots aim for the third,
wires mode=margin || || because it gives the safest margin from the back edge of the ship.
wires mode=below || || Then the wire pays out, pulling against huge machines below the deck that soak up the jet's energy.
wires mode=stop || [20+ TONNES] || The system is built to stop a jet weighing more than twenty tonnes,
wires mode=stop speed=1 || [240 KM/H] || coming in at around two hundred and forty kilometres an hour,
wires mode=stop dist=1 || [~100 M] || in roughly a hundred metres.
wires mode=pile || [2 SECONDS] || In about two seconds.
wires mode=beat || || Those cables take a terrible beating,
wires mode=inspect || INSPECT · REPLACE || so the crew inspect them constantly and replace them whenever wear and maintenance rules say so.
# --- 4. Why full throttle?
throttle || WHY FULL POWER? || So, why push to full power at the exact moment you land?
bolter mode=miss || || Because sometimes the hook misses every wire.
bolter mode=miss label=1 || "BOLTER" || That's called a bolter.
bolter mode=low || || If the engines were at low power, the jet could roll off the end of the deck into the sea.
bolter mode=go || GO AROUND || At full power, it simply takes off again, flies around, and tries one more time.
angled mode=1 || [~9°] ANGLED DECK || That's also why the landing area is angled, about nine degrees off to the side.
angled mode=2 || || A jet that misses flies off past the bow, instead of crashing into the planes parked at the front.
barricade step=1 || || And if a jet can't land normally at all, say its hook is damaged,
barricade step=2 || THE BARRICADE || the crew can raise a giant net across the deck, called the barricade, and catch the plane in it.
