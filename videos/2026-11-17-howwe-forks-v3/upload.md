## Description (paste as is)

Fork and knife. Chopsticks. Your right hand and a piece of bread. Same hunger, same food, completely different tools. So why did humanity never agree on how to eat?

The answer comes down to one question: where does the cutting happen? In the kitchen, at the table, or in the bread itself? From Chinese kitchens and Korean silver chopsticks to a scandalous golden fork in Venice, a cardinal's blunt knives, Ethiopian gursha and a Thai royal court, this is the story of three brilliant answers to the same problem. And why one of them may even be shaping your teeth.

None of these tools is better. Each one is a smart answer to a different problem.
