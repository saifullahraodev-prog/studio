## Style block (in every prompt)

> Premium editorial documentary illustration in the style of a high-end illustrated magazine or museum exhibition plate. Clean, slightly hand-drawn charcoal outlines (#34332F) of one consistent medium weight; flat muted colours with subtle soft shading and a fine paper grain; warm paper-cream ground (#F3EFE5) and soft sand (#D7D0C1); controlled accents of muted blue (#718A91) and terracotta (#A66D51). Natural proportions, believable individual people with restrained expressions, elegant balanced composition, detailed but uncluttered. 16:9 frame.
>
> Avoid: photorealism, 3D render, anime, children's cartoon, glossy vector clip-art, saturated or neon colours, any text or lettering, watermarks, extra fingers, stereotyped costume, mixing unrelated cultures.


The one dark scene (the reveal, scene with three spotlights) swaps the ground to charcoal; everything else stays on cream.
