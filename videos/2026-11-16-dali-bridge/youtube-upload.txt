DESCRIPTION (paste everything between the lines)
---------------------------------------------------------------------
At 1:25 in the morning on 26 March 2024, the container ship Dali lost all power, only about three ship lengths from Baltimore's Francis Scott Key Bridge. Four minutes later, the bridge was in the river. Six road workers lost their lives.

Twenty months later, the investigation found where it started: a tiny plastic wire label, put in the wrong place when the ship was built. This is the story of that night, why one hit brought down the whole bridge, the disaster in 1980 that should have prevented it, and what is changing now.

📊 KEY FACTS
• Ship: Dali, 300 m container ship, built 2015 · 4,700 containers aboard
• Blackout: about 1:25 a.m., roughly three ship lengths from the bridge
• Impact: about 1:29 a.m., at around 7 knots · the bridge fell in about 30 seconds
• Police stopped traffic at both ends in about 90 seconds
• NTSB final report (Nov 2025): a loose signal wire, caused by a misplaced wire label
• Channel fully reopened: 10 June 2024 · new bridge estimate: $4.3–5.2 billion, late 2030

We remember the six road workers who died that night.
