## How the Ship Guy is used (so it doesn't feel repetitive)

1. **He's part of the story, not a sticker.** He digs next to the giant bow and drives the little digger. He's on the bridge with his coffee when the ship hits the bank, rides the dredger and hangs off a tug rope. During the 3 months in the lake, he's fishing off the deck.
2. **Running gags** that come back:
   - **The little yellow digger**: the opening, the "joke of the year" part, and the new canal lane at the end.
   - **The hat flies off** at every big shock: the crash, 369 ships, $916 million.
   - **The coffee mug**: he drops it at the crash (7:41) and finally drinks it calmly on the canal bank in the last shot.
3. **Corner reactions on the numbers:** his jaw drops at the Empire State comparison, he faints at the $916 million invoice, and he cheers when the deal is signed.
4. **A new cast from the same puppet:** the two canal pilots (caps) and the captain (captain's hat), so the bridge scenes have real people in them.
5. **His mouth moves only while your voice is speaking** in host shots, and he blinks and breathes in every shot.
