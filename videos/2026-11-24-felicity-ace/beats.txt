# --- 6. Why it wouldn't go out
guy act=think bg=paper label=WHY? || || So why was this fire so hard to stop?
why7 mode=open || [1] ONE BIG OPEN DECK || First, a car deck is one giant open space.
why7 mode=spread || NO WALLS · LOTS OF AIR || There are no walls between the cars to stop a fire spreading, and air can flow along the whole deck.
why7 mode=burn || [2] FULL OF FUEL || Second, every car is full of things that burn. Fuel, plastic, foam seats, tyres.
why7 mode=many || || Thousands of cars means thousands of fires waiting to happen.
ev7 || [3] ELECTRIC CARS || And third, the electric cars.
why7 mode=battery || THERMAL RUNAWAY || A lithium-ion battery that overheats can go into what's called thermal runaway.
why7 mode=chain || || One cell heats up, sets off the next one, and the next one,
why7 mode=relight || || and the battery keeps feeding its own fire. It can even reignite after it looks like it's out.
why7 mode=report || [NOT PREPARED] || The investigators said it plainly. Car carriers, at the time, were not prepared to put out fires on electric and hybrid cars.
why7 mode=report2 || || There was no special equipment, and no special procedure.
