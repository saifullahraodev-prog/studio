In 1988, fighter jets set the biggest ship ever built on fire. Then it was rebuilt and sailed again under four new names.

Seawise Giant was 458 metres long, taller than the Petronas Towers if you stood it on its end. It was too big for the Suez Canal, the Panama Canal and even the English Channel when fully loaded, and it needed about 9 km to stop. In this video: how a supertanker nobody wanted became the longest ship in history, how it survived a missile strike in the Iran–Iraq Tanker War, and why the world decided never to build one like it again.
