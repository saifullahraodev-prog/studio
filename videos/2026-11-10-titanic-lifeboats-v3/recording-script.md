# Recording script: Short #1 (Titanic lifeboats), about 30 seconds

Read it in one take, top to bottom. Leave a short breath between lines (the gaps below).
If you stumble, pause, then just say the whole line again; I'll keep the last clean take.

---

**[THE INSPECTOR: calm but urgent, like you're right there]**

It's two-oh-five a.m. The deck is tilting. And you're standing in line for a lifeboat.

*(short pause)*

**[NARRATOR: talking to "you", tense]**

You count the boats. Then you count the people.

*(short pause)*

The math doesn't work. There are seats for about half of you.

*(short pause)*

An officer waves the next group in. Women and children only.

*(short pause)*

Then you see it. Earlier boats rowed away half empty.

*(a slightly longer pause: let it land)*

The worst part? It was all legal.

The rulebook stopped at ships less than a quarter this size.

*(pause)*

**[THE INSPECTOR: firm, slower, the "lesson" line]**

And that's why, today, every ship must carry a lifeboat or life-raft seat for everyone on board.

---

## Tips
- Pace: a little faster than normal talking, but every word clear (about 30 seconds total).
- Stress the words in **bold** if you like: two-oh-five · **tilting** · **half** · **Women and children only** · **half empty** · **legal** · **quarter** · **today** · **everyone**.
- You can use one voice for both the Inspector and the narrator, or give the Inspector a slightly
  different tone. Both work.
- Record somewhere quiet (a room with curtains or a closet is great). Keep the phone or mic about a hand's
  width (15–20 cm) from your mouth.
- Any format is fine: a phone voice memo (.m4a), .mp3 or .wav. Just send me the file.
