# Short #1 revision 2 ("v2" in the owner's review): POV survival script. DRAFT, NOT YET APPROVED

Target 30-40 s (~105 words). The Inspector opens and closes. Numbers are shown on screen, not read out.

1. (Inspector, night deck, tilting) It's two-oh-five a.m. The deck is tilting. And you're standing in line for a lifeboat.
2. You count the boats. Then you count the people.   [visual: 20 boats beside a crowd labelled 2,200]
3. The math doesn't work. There are seats for about half of you.
4. An officer waves the next group in. Women and children only.
5. Then you see it: earlier boats rowed away half empty.
6. The worst part? It was all legal.
7. The rulebook stopped at ships less than a quarter this size.   [visual: page "10,000 tons and upwards: 16 boats" next to the 46,328-ton ship]
8. (Inspector, today) And that's why, today, every ship must carry a lifeboat or life-raft seat for everyone on board.
