## Description
In 1912 the Titanic carried more lifeboats than the law asked for, and still had seats for only about half the people on board. The British lifeboat rules dated from 1894, and their biggest category was "10,000 tons and upwards". Titanic was 46,328 tons.

Less than two years later, the first SOLAS treaty (Safety of Life at Sea, London 1914) required lifeboat space for everyone on board, and that's why, today, passenger ships on international voyages must carry a lifeboat or life-raft seat for every person.

Rules written in disaster. Every safety rule has a story.
