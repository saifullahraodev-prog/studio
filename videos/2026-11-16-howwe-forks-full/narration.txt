In France, the fork reached the royal court in the early sixteen hundreds. And that's when the knife changed too.

The story goes that Cardinal Richelieu was so annoyed by guests picking their teeth with knife points, that he had his knives ground round.

With no point on the knife, the fork had to do the spearing.

Chinese cooks did the cutting in the kitchen. By the time food reached the table, it was already bite-sized.
