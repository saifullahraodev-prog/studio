## 1. Look (pick one: A / B / C)
| | A · Paper Atlas (recommended) | B · Riso Print | C · Museum Exhibit |
|---|---|---|---|
| World | Old map paper with a faint graticule, ink linework, soft watercolour washes, torn-paper split seam | Two-ink risograph print: halftone dots, grain, misregistered overprint | Dark gallery wall, objects on plinths under spotlights, exhibit label cards |
| Palette | paper #EFE4CC, ink #2A2A35, west red #C8553D, east blue #2E6F8E, gold #D9A441 | paper #F3EEE3, pink #FF4F8B, blue #2D5BD0, yellow #FFC83D | wall #1E2B28, cream #F4EEDF, gold #C9A24A |
| Type | Fraunces (titles), Caveat (handwritten notes) | Archivo Black + Space Grotesk | Playfair Display + IBM Plex Mono |
| Feels like | A curious traveller's notebook | A trendy zine or poster | A premium documentary |
| Strengths | Warm and global; the split seam and map insets *are* the channel idea; handwritten notes allow asides and humour; doesn't look like Ship Guy (clean white/blue) or Backwards (kraft cardboard) | Most eye-catching in the feed; very cheap to draw (2 colours); clearly not AI-template | Feels serious and trustworthy; "exhibit" framing suits myth-checks |
| Weaknesses | Washes and paper need restraint or frames get busy | Can feel cold over 12 minutes; less room for maps and nuance; trend may date | Dark and static; spotlight-on-plinth gets repetitive; weakest thumbnail at feed size |

**Rule for all options:** Ship Guy is clean flat vector on white/blue, and Backwards is kraft cardboard. How We Ended Up must look like neither.

## 2. Fixed screen grammar (all looks)
- **Split screen** (here vs there, or then vs now) is the signature shot. It appears in every video and every thumbnail.
- **Map insets** in the top corners show which region each side is.
- **Chapter tag** (THE FORGOTTEN PROBLEM / THE FORK IN THE ROAD / THE STORY YOU HEARD / BACK TO YOUR TABLE) plus **one big keyword** per beat.
- **Thumbnails:** two objects split, at most 3 words, and the same layout every video.
- **Format:** 16:9 long-form (10–16 min) first. Shorts are 9:16 cut-downs.

## 3. Still to decide after the look is picked
- Characters: faceless objects only, or simple people illustrations per region (respectful, no caricature).
- Motion style: paper slides and parallax, or ink-draw-on, or print-roll.
- Sound identity: a signature sound per split (page turn / print thunk / gallery footstep).
- Narration: owner's voice vs TTS at launch (plan says human voice by video ~10).
